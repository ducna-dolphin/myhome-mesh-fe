Branch `feat/internet-redesign`, mock mode (MSW, `vite` dev server), driven with `agent-browser`

## Summary

The app is functionally complete and visually close to XD, but it **feels like a web page, not a
native sheet-based app**: every overlay pops in/out with no exit, the grabber suggests a swipe that
does nothing, toasts are invisible whenever a sheet is open (so "copied"/"saved" feedback is lost),
and the signature measurement moment uses a generic spinner. Two layout/hit-target bugs block
one-handed use (Wi-Fi card password area is dead, tabs overflow at 360 px).

Top 5: F01 toast under sheets · F02 dead tap area on Wi-Fi card · F03 360 px horizontal scroll ·
F09 sheet has no exit / swipe-dismiss · F19 measurement moment lacks its signature motion.

## Cross-cutting recommendations

- **Motion tokens** (durations 100/150/250/350 ms, exit ≈ 75 %, `--ease-out-quart|quint|expo`,
  `--ease-exit`, switch-only `--ease-overshoot`, stagger step) in `tokens.css`, zeroed under
  `prefers-reduced-motion`; global reduced-motion rule also zeroes delays.
- **One exit helper** (`runExit`) reading the element's computed animation time, resolving on
  `animationend` with a timeout fallback, synchronous when nothing animates (jsdom, reduced motion).
- **Overlays**: sheet + dialog share presence logic (render the last content during exit).
- **Feedback**: toasts above modals; inline confirmations for actions that happen inside sheets.
